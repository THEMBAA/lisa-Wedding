The Story of Their Love

The Meeting

Ukuhlangana · Kusangana

Their story began on 20 September 2025 at a musical art event curated by a mutual friend and Lisa's brother. Although Themba had seen Lisa from time to time and already knew who she was, she had never really paid much attention to him. That changed when she arrived at the event and noticed him playing music. Her first thought was simply that he had great taste in music. Before the event began, Themba invited everyone to share a song that could be played while they waited. Later that evening, as Lisa was leaving, their paths crossed and she said goodbye. He already knew her name, but Lisa, being terrible with names, admitted that she couldn't remember his. With a smile, Themba told her she could call him "that mysterious guy." She laughed and said she would do exactly that the next time they crossed paths.

What began with music & a forgotten name…

The Beginning

Ukuqala · Kutanga

A few days later, Lisa got Themba's number through something her brother needed her to contact him about, and what started as an ordinary conversation soon became something neither of them expected. The more they spoke, the more naturally they were drawn to one another. Themba's thoughtful gestures — like bringing Lisa little gifts when he returned from work — slowly revealed the depth of his heart, while their conversations uncovered how beautifully aligned they were in the things that mattered most: their faith in God, their love for family, and their vision for life. What makes their story special is that Themba had noticed Lisa long before she noticed him, yet when the right moment finally came, their paths crossed in a way that felt almost perfectly timed. What began with music, a forgotten name and a mysterious guy became a love story they now get to call their own.
